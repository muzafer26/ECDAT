The Difference

Traditional Discovery

  • Raw alerts: "RSA found in file.java"
  • Opaque severity scores without rationale
  • Guesses missing properties like bit-length
  • No operational deployment context
  • Migration left to guesswork

The ECDAT Approach

  • What evidence? Auditable source context
  • What role? Role-aware primitive classification
  • What risk? Classical status and quantum exposure
  • How uncertain? Explicitly flags missing evidence
  • What target? NIST FIPS-approved PQC candidates
  • What review? Mandatory gates before sign-off
